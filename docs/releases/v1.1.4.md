# Odrive-Wheel v1.1.4 — FFB fixes and hands-off oscillation protection

Changes since v1.1.3.

## Hands-off oscillation protection

New optional protection helps reduce oscillations that can build up when you
let go of the wheel with Force Feedback active. When it detects an oscillation,
it reduces the game's force and applies damping, then gradually restores normal
operation when it detects that you have regained control.

- Off by default, with adjustable strength in the FFB Wheel tab.
- Can use the inertia measured in Performance Test to help distinguish a free
  wheel from movements made by the driver.
- Inertia test results now show measurement quality and can be applied directly
  to the protection settings.
- An overlay indicator shows when protection is acting. Guided tests and
  diagnostics are available in advanced mode.

## Force Feedback fixes in games

- Fixed interpretation of the direction reported by games, which could produce
  force in the wrong direction with certain effect combinations.
- Fixed an issue where parameters sent by a game for a second axis could
  overwrite the wheel's parameters and cancel spring or damping effects.
- Spring, Damper, Friction and Inertia now respect the individual effect gain
  requested by the game, including zero gain.
- Pause/Continue preserve remaining playback time, start delay and effect phase,
  rather than allowing them to advance during a pause.
- Updating an effect no longer incorrectly resets its playback clock. Frequency
  changes preserve periodic effect phase with 1 ms resolution.
- Corrected FFB status reporting and the distinction between pausing effects and
  disabling their output. New parameters do not reactivate disabled output;
  the reset command removes effects, clears pause and re-enables the actuators.
- Prevented an artificial inertia impulse on the first velocity reading.

## Calibration and robustness

- On power-up or a reset-button restart, the board now waits two seconds before
  initialization to allow the power supply to settle. Software reboots do not
  incur this extra delay.
- Fixed incremental encoder calibration with a Z index and an offset mechanical
  center, which could cause incorrect alignment and different force behavior
  after restarting the board.
- Invalid parameters are handled safely, and invalid torque commands are
  blocked before reaching the motor.
- Improved USB FFB message validation to prevent incomplete reports from being
  processed.
- Updated default spinout-detection settings are less sensitive to brief power
  peaks during impacts or strong FFB. Previously saved settings are not
  automatically overwritten.

## Motor electrical response settings

New options anticipate force response, compensate for motion using filtered
velocity, and smooth small current corrections. Depending on the motor and its
calibration, these can help balance force response against buzzing or vibration.

The **Motor electrical response** group explains what each option does in
practice, which options are alternatives, and which takes priority. The original
ODrive alternatives are available in advanced mode.

## Clearer overlay and configuration

- Axis, motor, encoder and controller errors appear in the overlay with their
  source, code and name. Error updates use HID, require no serial connection,
  and continue even when the charts are paused.
- New visual current smoothing makes the overlay chart easier to read without
  changing the wheel's force.
- Calibration Lockin, Slew rate / curve, and the anti-oscillation test/diagnostic
  panel are hidden in simplified mode.
- While programming the MT6835's memory, the configurator displays the waiting
  period and suspends sensor queries.

## Updating

Settings saved on the board are preserved, as are the existing position and
force inversion options. No third inversion option has been added.

Because conditional effects now respect the individual gain sent by the game,
spring, damping, friction and inertia strength may change in games that use
this control.

Use the firmware with the updated configurator: the HTML FFB and performance
tests have been adjusted to match the stricter USB message validation.

Some FFB fixes were adapted from TelksBr's contributions to ODrive-Wheel-Forge.
